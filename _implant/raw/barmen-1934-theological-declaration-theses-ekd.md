# Barmen 1934 — Theological Declaration, theses 1, 2 and 5 (EKD English text)

Source: Confessional Synod of the German Evangelical Church, Barmen, 29–31 May 1934, "Theological Declaration concerning the present situation of the German Evangelical Church" (Barmer Theologische Erklärung), English text as published by the Evangelical Church in Germany (EKD), which gives as its source the Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.) Book of Confessions.
Original: https://www.ekd.de/en/The-Barmen-Declaration-303.htm (translation copyrighted by its publisher; excerpts only)
Retrieved: 2026-10-08 (fetched with curl, converted to text; quotations copied from it)

> "The Confessional Synod of the German Evangelical Church met in Barmen, May 29-31 1934."  (appeal)

> "Jesus Christ, as he is attested for us in holy scripture, is the one Word of God which we have to hear and which we have to trust and obey in life and in death."  (thesis 1)

> "We reject the false doctrine, as though the church could and would have to acknowledge as a source of its proclamation, apart from and besides this one Word of God, still other events and powers, figures and truths, as God's revelation."  (thesis 1)

> "We reject the false doctrine, as though there were areas of our life in which we would not belong to Jesus Christ, but to other lords - areas in which we would not need justification and sanctification through him."  (thesis 2)

> "We reject the false doctrine, as though the state, over and beyond its special commission, should and could become the single and totalitarian order of human life, thus fulfilling the church's vocation as well."  (thesis 5)

Relied on for: date of the synod and the wording of theses 1, 2 and 5 on the Barth page. Barth's authorship of the draft is from Britannica (Torrance) and Krötke 2006, not from this text.
Context: six theses, each a Bible verse, an affirmation and a rejection ("We reject the false doctrine ..."), framed by an appeal to congregations and a preamble naming the "German Christians".
