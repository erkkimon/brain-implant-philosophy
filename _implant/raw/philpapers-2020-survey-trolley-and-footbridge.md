Source: David Bourget & David J. Chalmers, 2020 PhilPapers Survey, results
  pages "Trolley problem (five straight ahead, one on side track, turn
  requires switching, what ought one do?): don't switch or switch?" and
  "Footbridge (pushing man off bridge will save five on track below, what
  ought one do?): push or don't push?", default view: population "Target
  group" ("Respondents from the primary target population."), AOS "All
  respondents". Paper: Bourget & Chalmers, "Philosophers on Philosophy:
  The 2020 PhilPapers Survey", Philosophers' Imprint 23 (2023),
  doi 10.3998/phimp.2109 (not read here).
