> "Intuitionistic logic can be succinctly described as classical logic without the Aristotelian law of excluded middle"  (§1)

> "Every intuitionistic proof of a closed statement of the form A ∨ B can be effectively transformed into an intuitionistic proof of A or an intuitionistic proof of B, and similarly for closed existential statements."  (§1)

Relied on for: what intuitionistic logic drops, and the existence property
