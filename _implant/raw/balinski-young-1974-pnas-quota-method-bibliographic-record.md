> "The problem of Congressional apportionment is explained together with a brief history of the methods used or considered for its solution. Reasons are given for rejecting the presently used method of equal proportions and for accepting a new method, the quota method, which is the unique method satisfying three essential axioms."  (abstract, as served in the Crossref record)

Relied on for: the existence, date and stated aim of the quota-method paper.
Context: Malkevitch's AMS column reports that the authors later rejected the
quota method because it does not avoid the population paradox (see
malkevitch-ams-feature-column-apportionment.md); Balinski and Young 1980, §7,
present the quota method as "Balinski and Young (1975)".
