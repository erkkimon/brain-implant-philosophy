# Kant 1785 — Groundwork, First Section: the good will shines "like a jewel" (Abbott translation)

Source: Immanuel Kant, Grundlegung zur Metaphysik der Sitten (1785), First
  Section; English: Fundamental Principles of the Metaphysic of Morals,
  trans. Thomas Kingsmill Abbott (Project Gutenberg eBook #5682).
Original: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5682 (plain text
  https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/5682/pg5682.txt); canonical locator
  Akademie-Ausgabe 4:394 (the locator Nelkin's SEP entry gives for this
  passage; the Gutenberg text carries no Academy pagination)
  (public domain)
Retrieved: 2026-09-27 (plain text downloaded and read)

> "A good will is good not because of what it performs or effects, not by its aptness for the attainment of some proposed end, but simply by virtue of the volition; that is, it is good in itself"  (Groundwork, First Section; Academy 4:394 per Nelkin, SEP §1)

> "Even if it should happen that, owing to special disfavour of fortune, or the niggardly provision of a step-motherly nature, this will should wholly lack power to accomplish its purpose, if with its greatest efforts it should yet achieve nothing, and there should remain only the good will (not, to be sure, a mere wish, but the summoning of all means in our power), then, like a jewel, it would still shine by its own light, as a thing which has its whole value in itself. Its usefulness or fruitlessness can neither add nor take away anything from this value."  (Groundwork, First Section; Academy 4:394 per Nelkin, SEP §1)

Relied on for: the Kantian source of the Control Principle as Nelkin
(SEP §1) and Nagel (1979) present it.
Context: the passage follows Kant's claim that moderation, self-control and
calm deliberation are not good without qualification, since "the coolness
of a villain" makes him more dangerous; it continues: "It would be, as it
were, only the setting to enable us to handle it the more conveniently in
common commerce". Nelkin quotes the same passage in Gregor's translation;
Nagel 1979 quotes it in another translation.
