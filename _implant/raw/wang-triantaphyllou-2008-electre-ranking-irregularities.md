> "Often times different MCDM methods may yield different answers to exactly the same problem [Triantaphyllou, 2000]!"  (§1)

> "In some earlier research it was found that the previous additive models might often exhibit cases of irregular ranking reversals under certain tests [Triantaphyllou, 2000 and 2001; Triantaphyllou and Mann, 1989; Belton and Gear, 1983]. However, the previous multiplicative models are immune to most of these ranking irregularities."  (§1)

> "The TOPSIS method was also found to suffer of the ranking irregularities related to the AHP and its additive variants (according to some unpublished results by the authors)."  (§1)

> "However, there is no legitimate reason why the optimal alternative should also be changed and why the original incomparable relation between two equally ranked alternatives should also be changed."  (discussion of the first ELECTRE example)

Relied on for: the claim that methods give different answers to the same
  problem, as these authors state it; the extension of ranking
  irregularities to ELECTRE and (by unpublished results) TOPSIS; the
  authors' normative premise behind the replace-with-a-worse-alternative
  test.
Context: "Additive models" here are the WSM, the AHP and the revised (ideal
  mode) AHP; "multiplicative" are the WPM and the multiplicative AHP of
  Barzilai and Lootsma and of Lootsma. The quoted "no legitimate reason"
  sentence follows the authors' ELECTRE II/III example in which alternative
  A1 is replaced by a worse one.
