> "Thus the emotivism that was his dominant view in the latter part of his life is vulnerable to objections that he himself had raised in an earlier incarnation, as was the error theory that he briefly espoused in 1922."  (preamble)

> "What “X is good” means is “Would that everyone desired X!”."  (§1)
