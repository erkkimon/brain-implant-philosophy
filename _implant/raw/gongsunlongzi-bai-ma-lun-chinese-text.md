# Gongsunlongzi ch. 2 "Bai ma lun" 白馬論 — Chinese text (excerpts)

Source: Gongsunlongzi 公孫龍子, chapter 2, "Bai ma lun di er" 白馬論第二, Chinese
  text as transcribed on Chinese Wikisource (page 公孫龍子/2, with the
  traditional commentary in small type omitted below). Pre-Han text; the
  attribution to Gongsun Long and the dating are discussed in the SEP
  "School of Names" excerpt (sep-school-names-fall-2024-white-horse.md).
Original: https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E5%85%AC%E5%AD%AB%E9%BE%8D%E5%AD%90/2
  (public domain, tagged PD-old on Wikisource). The Chinese Text Project
  (https://ctext.org/gongsunlongzi/bai-ma-lun) hosts the same chapter but
  refused automated retrieval on 2026-10-01 and was not used.
Retrieved: 2026-10-01 (raw wikitext via action=raw; each line below checked
  as a substring of the fetched text). No English translation is given here;
  an English rendering of the five arguments, with Fraser's numbering, is in
  the SEP excerpt.

> "「白馬非馬，可乎？」曰：「可。」"  (opening)

> "馬者，所以命形也；白者，所以命色也；命色者，非命形也。故曰白馬非馬。"  (argument 1)

> "求馬，黃、黑馬皆可致；求白馬，黃、黑馬不可致。"  (argument 2)

> "白馬者，馬與白也，白與馬也，馬與白馬也。故曰白馬非馬也。"  (argument 3)

> "以黃馬爲非馬，而以白馬爲有馬，此飛者入池而棺椁異處，此天下之悖言亂辭也。"  (argument 4)

> "馬未與白爲馬，白未與馬爲白。合白與馬，復名白馬。是相與以不相與爲名，未可。故曰白馬非馬，未可。"  (objector, before argument 4)

> "白者不定所白，忘之而可也。"  (argument 5, opening)

> "白馬者言白，定所白也。定所白者，非白也。"  (argument 5, continued after commentary)

> "無去者，非有去也。故曰白馬非馬。"  (argument 5, end)


Relied on for: the wording of the thesis and its opening question (可乎), the
first argument (naming shape vs. naming colour), the seeking-a-horse
argument, the "horse combined with white" argument, the yellow-horse
argument, and the 定所白 / 去取 argument.
Context: the chapter is a dialogue between an unnamed proponent and an
objector; the objector's turns (e.g. 有白馬，不可謂無馬也) are interleaved with
the replies quoted here.
