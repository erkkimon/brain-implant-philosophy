> "In 1913 Jourdain proposed the card paradox. This was a card on one side of which was printed:- The sentence on the other side of this card is TRUE. On the other side of the card the sentence read:- The sentence on the other side of this card is FALSE."  (paragraph on Jourdain's logical work; the two card sentences are set on separate lines in the original)

> "Inspired by Russell, Jourdain worked mainly in mathematical logic."  (same paragraph)

Relied on for: the date 1913 and Jourdain's card wording, as this
secondary source reports them; it is also the reference the Wikipedia
"Card paradox" article cites.
Context: the biography names no publication for the 1913 card; the same
paragraph mentions Jourdain's articles on Cantor's transfinite numbers
(1906–1913) and the paradox of the largest ordinal. A search of
Jourdain's 1913 Monist "A Correction and Some Remarks" (archive.org
jstor-27900419) found no card.
