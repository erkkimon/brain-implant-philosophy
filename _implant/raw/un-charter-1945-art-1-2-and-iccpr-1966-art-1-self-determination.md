> "To develop friendly relations among nations based on respect for the principle of equal rights and self-determination of peoples, and to take other appropriate measures to strengthen universal peace;"  (UN Charter, Art. 1(2))

> "All peoples have the right of self-determination. By virtue of that right they freely determine their political status and freely pursue their economic, social and cultural development."  (ICCPR, Art. 1(1))

> "All peoples may, for their own ends, freely dispose of their natural wealth and resources without prejudice to any obligations arising out of international economic co-operation, based upon the principle of mutual benefit, and international law."  (ICCPR, Art. 1(2))

Relied on for: the wording of self-determination of peoples in the two
  instruments.
Context: Charter Art. 1 lists four purposes; the first is international
  peace and security. ICCPR Art. 1(2) continues "In no case may a people be
  deprived of its own means of subsistence." The date and place of signature
  are from the UN's Charter overview page
  (https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter): "It was signed on 26 June
  1945, in San Francisco".
