# ICCPR (1966) — preamble and Articles 18, 19, 21, 22, 25: the civil and political freedoms listed

Source: International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights, adopted 16
  December 1966 by General Assembly resolution 2200A (XXI); preamble
  (third recital) and Part III, Articles 18(1), 19(1)–(3), 21, 22(1), 25.

> "Recognizing that, in accordance with the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the ideal of free human beings enjoying civil and political freedom and freedom from fear and want can only be achieved if conditions are created whereby everyone may enjoy his civil and political rights, as well as his economic, social and cultural rights,"  (preamble)

> "1. Everyone shall have the right to freedom of thought, conscience and religion."  (Art. 18(1), first sentence)

> "1. Everyone shall have the right to hold opinions without interference."  (Art. 19(1))

> "2. Everyone shall have the right to freedom of expression; this right shall include freedom to seek, receive and impart information and ideas of all kinds, regardless of frontiers, either orally, in writing or in print, in the form of art, or through any other media of his choice."  (Art. 19(2))

> "It may therefore be subject to certain restrictions, but these shall only be such as are provided by law and are necessary:"  (Art. 19(3), followed by (a) the rights or reputations of others and (b) national security, public order, public health or morals)

> "The right of peaceful assembly shall be recognized."  (Art. 21, first sentence)

> "1. Everyone shall have the right to freedom of association with others, including the right to form and join trade unions for the protection of his interests."  (Art. 22(1))

> "Every citizen shall have the right and the opportunity, without any of the distinctions mentioned in article 2 and without unreasonable restrictions:"  (Art. 25, chapeau)

> "(a) To take part in the conduct of public affairs, directly or through freely chosen representatives;"  (Art. 25(a))

> "(b) To vote and to be elected at genuine periodic elections which shall be by universal and equal suffrage and shall be held by secret ballot, guaranteeing the free expression of the will of the electors;"  (Art. 25(b))

Relied on for: the list of civil and political freedoms in the Covenant's
  own wording; the restriction clause of Art. 19(3).
Context: Art. 20 follows Art. 19 and requires prohibition by law of war
  propaganda and of advocacy of hatred that constitutes incitement. Art.
  21 and 22(2) carry restriction clauses parallel to Art. 19(3). Art. 1
  (self-determination of peoples) is excerpted separately in
