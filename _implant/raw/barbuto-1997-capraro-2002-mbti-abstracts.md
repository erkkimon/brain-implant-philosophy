> "Specifically, the dichotomous nature of the indices is criticized as is its operationalization of Jung's psychological types."  (Barbuto 1997, abstract)

> "This paper argues that Jung's stated intentions for understanding individual behavior suggest that personality variables exist in various levels of consciousness and unconsciousness which require study to consider the proportions with which each exists."  (Barbuto 1997, abstract)

> "The paper also considers a reconstruction of the measure of Jung's psychological types and reconsideration of the descriptions and characteristics of each personality function measured."  (Barbuto 1997, abstract)
