# mkgandhi.org chronology — Gandhi's dates (birth, London, South Africa, satyagraha, Hind Swaraj, return, Dandi, death)

Source: "Chronology", Events in years 1869–1948, mkgandhi.org (site

  Research Foundation), table rows Year | Month | Date | Event.
Original: https://www.mkgandhi.org/chrono/chronology1.php
  (website text; excerpts only)
Retrieved: 2026-10-08 (HTML table fetched and parsed row by row; month

> "Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi born of a Bania (Vaishya or trading caste) family at Porbunder, Kathiawar, the youngest of the three sons of Karamchand alias Kaba Gandhi, Prime Minister successively in Porbunder, Rajkot and Vankaner States, and his fourth wife Putlibai"  (1869 October 2)

> "Sails for England."  (1888 September 4)

> "Called to the Bar."  (1891 June 10)

> "Leaves for South Africa, being engaged by a Muslim firm for legal work."  (1893 April)

> "Studies religious literature including the Bible, he Koran and Tolstoy’s The Kingdom of God is Within You."  (1894 September; "he" as printed)

> "Reads Ruskin’s Unto This Last : founds Phoenix Settlement near Durban (Natal);"  (1904)

> "Adopts word : ‘Satyagraha’ in place of ‘Passive Resistance’."  (1908 January 10)

> "Writes to Tolstoy regarding Passive Resistance movement."  (1909 October 1)

> "Leaves England for South Africa. Writes Hind Swaraj on board "s. s. Kildonan Castle"."  (1909 November 13)

> "Tolstoy replies: question of Passive Resistance of greatest importance not only for India but for humanity."  (1910 May 8)

> "Founds Tolstoy Farm."  (1910 [May] 30)

> "Reaches India Awarded Kaiser-i-Hind Gold Medal for Ambulance services."  (1915 January 9)

> "Assumes editorship of the English weekly, Young India;"  (1919 October)

> "Commences march to Dandi sea-beach, where he ceremoniously picks up salt (April 6)."  (1930 March 12)

> "Founds the weekly paper Harijan, published in English and Hindi."  (1933 February 11)

> "Is assassinated on way to evening prayer."  (1948 January 30)
