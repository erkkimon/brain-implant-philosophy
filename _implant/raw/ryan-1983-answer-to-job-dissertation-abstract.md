> "Answer to Job is a paradigm of the individuation process, the lens through which all of Jung's theories may be viewed."  (abstract)

> "The problem of evil was a central concern of Jung's life and work. It is a major theme in Answer to Job; a point which received a great deal of criticism from religious thinkers."  (abstract)

> "It also examines Jung's objection to the concept of the privatio boni as an explanation for the cause of evil. It attempts to answer his objections in light of Christian tradition."  (abstract)

Relied on for: a theological reading that ties the book to the problem of evil and replies to Jung's objection to the privatio boni (works/answer-to-job.md).
