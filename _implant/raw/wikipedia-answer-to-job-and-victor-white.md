"Answer to Job":

> "He interprets the 1950 papal dogma of the Assumption of Mary as easing this transition toward completeness by re-emphasizing the feminine dimension of God."  (lead)

> "Author Joyce Carol Oates described Answer to Job in her review "Legendary Jung" as Jung's most important work."  (Reception)

"Victor White (priest)":

> "He was initially deeply attracted to Jung's psychology, but when Jung's Answer to Job was published in English, he gave it a very critical review."  (lead)

> "At the same time, Jung was quite critical of White, for example, over his commitment to the doctrine of privatio boni as means of understanding the problem of evil."  (body)

> "White published a review of Answer to Job in the journal Blackfriars in March 1955. It was clear from this review that by this stage, White's feelings on the book were ambivalent, and he even seems to have had mixed feelings about his publication of the review, wondering how Jung would take the review."  (body)
