> "Blending Christian mysticism, Eastern philosophy, and psychological insight, his teachings invite people to awaken from conditioned patterns of fear and illusion into a life of presence, freedom, and love."

> "Afterward, he earned a master's in counseling from Loyola University, Chicago, and a postgraduate degree in spiritual theology from Gregorian University in Rome."

> "His first exposure to the power of awareness came from retreats with the Vipassana teacher S. N. Goenka."

> "Tony died suddenly of a heart attack in 1987 at the age of fifty-five in New York City."

> "the biography written by his younger brother, Bill de Mello, called The Happy Wanderer."

> "Sadhana Institute was founded in Pune by the late Tony de Mello, S.J. in 1973 and was relocated five years later to Lonavla, a picturesque hill station nestled in the Western Ghats. It was originally conceived as a centre for spirituality for the training of spiritual guides and retreat masters."

> "And from the first days of Sadhana there was also an emphasis on the integration of Christian spirituality with the Indian heritage."
