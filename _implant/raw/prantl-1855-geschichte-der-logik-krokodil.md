# Prantl 1855 — the crocodile among the Stoic sophisms (Geschichte der Logik I)

Source: Carl Prantl, Geschichte der Logik im Abendlande, vol. 1 (Leipzig:
  S. Hirzel, 1855), ch. VI "Die Stoiker", p. 493, items 5–6 and nn. 216–217.
Original: https://archive.org/details/geschichtederlog01pran (University of
  Toronto scan; public domain). Excerpts only.
Retrieved: 2026-10-01 (archive.org OCR text read; German passages copied,
  line-break hyphens joined; the Greek in the OCR is garbled and is not
  quoted)

> "Ein Krokodil hat ein Kind geraubt und verspricht dem Vater desselben die Zurückgabe, wofern er errathe, welchen Entschluss betreffs der Rückgabe oder Nicht-Rückgabe das Krokodil gefasst habe."  (p. 493, item 5)

> "so ist das Krokodil rathlos, was es thun solle"  (p. 493, of the father guessing non-return; OCR "Rälh nun der Vater auf Nicht-Rückgabe" precedes)

> "In einer anderen Version sind statt des Krokodils Seeräuber genannt"  (p. 493, item 5)

> "eine andere Wendung hievon, welche aber wenigstens später auch den eigenen Namen"  (p. 493, item 6, introducing the next sophism, whose Greek name the OCR garbles; n. 217 ties it to Gellius V.10)

> "Vollständig gibt diesen Fangschluss, welcher aus begreiflichen Gründen in der Schultradition den Sophisten zugeschrieben wird, Gell. V, 10."  (n. 217)

Relied on for: Prantl's placing of the crocodile among Stoic sophisms; his
statement of the case and of the father's alternative (guessing return);
the pirate variant he reports; his treatment of Gellius V.10 (Protagoras
and Euathlus) as "another turn" of it.
Context: p. 493 lists Stoic sophisms (item 4 is the Reaper). Note 216 cites
Diogenes Laertius (VII.44, 82), scholia on Hermogenes (Walz IV, pp. 154,
170) and Lucian's Vitarum auctio for the name. MathWorld cites this page
("Vol. 1 ... p. 493").
English gist (this implant's translation, not a quotation): a crocodile has
stolen a child and promises the father its return if he guesses what the
crocodile has decided about returning it; if the father guesses
non-return, the crocodile is at a loss what to do.
