> "For it is a voluntary act: and of the voluntary acts of every man, the object is some Good To Himselfe."  (ch. XIV, on transferring or renouncing a right)

> "For no man giveth, but with intention of Good to himselfe; because Gift is Voluntary; and of all Voluntary Acts, the Object is to every man his own Good;"  (ch. XV, the fourth law of nature)

Relied on for: the sentences of Hobbes's on which psychological-egoist

Context: ch. XIV's sentence explains why a man who transfers a right does
so "in consideration of some Right reciprocally transferred to himselfe;
or for some other good he hopeth for thereby"; ch. XV's continues that
without this there "will be no beginning of benevolence, or trust".
