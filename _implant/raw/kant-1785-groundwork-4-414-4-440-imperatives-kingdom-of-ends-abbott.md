# Kant 1785 — Groundwork 4:414–4:440: hypothetical and categorical imperatives, the one categorical imperative, the kingdom of ends, price and dignity, autonomy (Abbott tr.)

Source: Immanuel Kant, *Fundamental Principles of the Metaphysic of Morals* (Grundlegung zur
  Metaphysik der Sitten, 1785), tr. Thomas Kingsmill Abbott, Project Gutenberg eBook #5682,
  Second Section. Abbott's text carries no Akademie pagination; each locator below was
  assigned by finding the German sentence in the Akademie-Ausgabe vol. IV as transcribed at
  korpora.org (https://korpora.org/kant/aa04/<page>.html), page and line given.
Original: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5682 (public domain translation);
  Akademie text: https://korpora.org/kant/aa04/ (Kant's Gesammelte Schriften, Bd. IV)
Retrieved: 2026-10-09 (Gutenberg plain text pg5682.txt fetched with curl, each quotation
  checked by script against it, whitespace normalised; korpora.org pages 414, 421, 430,
  432–434, 436, 439, 440 fetched and the German anchor of each passage found)

> "Now all imperatives command either hypothetically or categorically. The former represent the practical necessity of a possible action as means to something else that is willed (or at least which one might possibly will). The categorical imperative would be that which represented an action as necessary of itself without reference to another end, i.e., as objectively necessary."  (4:414, ll. 12–17: "Alle Imperativen nun gebieten entweder hypothetisch, oder kategorisch.")

> "There is therefore but one categorical imperative, namely, this: Act only on that maxim whereby thou canst at the same time will that it should become a universal law."  (4:421, ll. 6–8: "Der kategorische Imperativ ist also nur ein einziger und zwar dieser")

> "By a kingdom I understand the union of different rational beings in a system by common laws."  (4:433, ll. 17–18: "Ich verstehe aber unter einem Reiche die systematische Verbindung")

> "It is certainly only an ideal."  (4:433, l. 32: "freilich nur ein Ideal")

> "A rational being belongs as a member to the kingdom of ends when, although giving universal laws in it, he is also himself subject to these laws. He belongs to it as sovereign when, while giving laws, he is not subject to the will of any other."  (4:433, l. 34 – 4:434)

> "Morality consists then in the reference of all action to the legislation which alone can render a kingdom of ends possible."  (4:434, l. 7: "Moralität besteht also in der Beziehung aller Handlung auf die Gesetzgebung")

> "In the kingdom of ends everything has either value or dignity. Whatever has a value can be replaced by something else which is equivalent; whatever, on the other hand, is above all value, and therefore admits of no equivalent, has a dignity."  (4:434, ll. 31–33: "Im Reiche der Zwecke hat alles entweder einen Preis, oder eine Würde"; Abbott renders "Preis" as "value")

> "Teleology considers nature as a kingdom of ends; ethics regards a possible kingdom of ends as a kingdom nature."  (4:436, footnote: "Teleologie erwägt die Natur als ein Reich der Zwecke"; Abbott's text reads "a kingdom nature")

> "Act according to the maxims of a member of a merely possible kingdom of ends legislating in it universally,"  (4:439, ll. 1–3: "handle nach Maximen eines allgemein gesetzgebenden Gliedes zu einem bloß möglichen Reiche der Zwecke")

> "Autonomy of the will is that property of it by which it is a law to itself (independently of any property of the objects of volition)."  (4:440, l. 16: "Autonomie des Willens ist die Beschaffenheit des Willens")

Relied on for: the hypothetical / categorical distinction; the single categorical imperative
(4:421); the definition of the kingdom of ends, member and sovereign; price ("value") and
dignity; the kingdom-of-ends formula (4:439); autonomy (4:440).
Context: the 4:421 sentence follows a footnote distinguishing a maxim (the subjective
principle of acting) from practical law (the objective principle); 4:433–434
follows the autonomy formula (4:431–432); the "quod tibi non vis fieri" footnote is at 4:430
(see kant-mill-tolstoy-on-love-commandment-golden-rule-nonresistance.md; the German "denke ja
nicht, daß hier das triviale: quod tibi non vis fieri etc." is on korpora.org page 430).
