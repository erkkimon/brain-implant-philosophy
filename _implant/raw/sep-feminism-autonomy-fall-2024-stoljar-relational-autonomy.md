> "Feminist philosophers typically reject the Kantian and Rawlsian conceptions of autonomy."  (§1)

> "The notion of self implicit in the Kantian and Rawlsian accounts is said to be “atomistic”; that is, it is abstracted from the social relations in which actual agents are embedded."  (§1)

> "“Relational autonomy” is the name that has been given to feminist reconceptualizations of the notion of autonomy."  (§1)

Relied on for: the feminist rejection of Kantian and Rawlsian conceptions
  of autonomy, the "atomistic" charge, and "relational autonomy" as the name
  for feminist reconceptualisations.
Context: §1 opens with Kant's conception and Rawls's original position as
