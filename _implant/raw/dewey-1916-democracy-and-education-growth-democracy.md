> "Our net conclusion is that life is development, and that developing, growing, is life."  (Ch. 4, "Education as Growth")

> "Since growth is the characteristic of life, education is all one with growing; it has no end beyond itself."  (Ch. 4, Summary)

> "A democracy is more than a form of government; it is primarily a mode of associated living, of conjoint communicated experience."  (Ch. 7, "The Democratic Conception in Education")

> "If we are willing to conceive education as the process of forming fundamental dispositions, intellectual and emotional, toward nature and fellow men, philosophy may even be defined as the general theory of education."  (Ch. 24, "Philosophy of Education")
