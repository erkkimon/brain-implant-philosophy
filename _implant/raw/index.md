# Raw

Undistilled source material: excerpts, survey rows, data — what knowledge
pages ground on and what future distillation reads. No frontmatter, no
links required, but orderly: kebab-case names that say what a file is,
listed here, pruned once distilled or proved worthless. Only excerpts,
never copies — see the convention
[Raw holds excerpts, not copies](../conventions/raw-holds-excerpts-not-copies.md).
brainpick excludes this folder from search results; grep it.

## Files

* `tversky-kahneman-1974-anchoring.md` — the defining sentences and the wheel-of-fortune figures, Science 1974, p. 1128.
* `klein-2014-many-labs-anchoring.md` — Many Labs 1 anchoring rows: four items, 36 samples, effect sizes with 99 % CIs.
* `gigerenzer-1996-narrow-norms.md` — the "vague heuristics" and "narrow norms" sentences of the reply to Kahneman & Tversky.
* `locke-essay-4-17-19-22-four-arguments.md` — Locke coining *argumentum ad verecundiam* and contrasting it with *ad judicium*.
* `aristotle-rhetoric-1-2-1356a-three-proofs.md` — the three proofs furnished by the speech; character as "the most effective means of proof" (Freese).
* `sep-fallacies-fall-2024-excerpts.md` — how the standard treatment, Locke, Walton's schemes and pragma-dialectics classify the appeal to authority.
* `sep-disagreement-fall-2024-goldman-novice-expert.md` — Goldman's five evidential sources for a novice judging experts, as reported by SEP.
