> "‘Natural law theory’ is a label that has been applied to theories of ethics, theories of politics, theories of civil law, and theories of religious morality."  (preamble)

> "We will be concerned only with natural law theories of ethics: while such views arguably have some interesting implications for law, politics, and religious morality, these implications will not be our focus here."  (preamble)

> "The fundamental thesis affirmed here by Aquinas is that the natural law is a participation in the eternal law (ST IaIIae 91, 2)."  (§1.1)

> "The eternal law, for Aquinas, is that rational plan by which all creation is ordered (ST IaIIae 91, 1)"  (§1.1)

> "whereas the paradigmatic natural law view involves a commitment to God’s existence."  (§1.4)
