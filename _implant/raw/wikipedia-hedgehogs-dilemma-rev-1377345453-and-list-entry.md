List of paradoxes, "Decision theory":

> "Hedgehog's dilemma: Despite goodwill, human intimacy cannot occur without substantial mutual harm."

Article, lead:

> "The hedgehog's dilemma, or sometimes the porcupine dilemma, is a metaphor about the challenges of human intimacy."

> "Though they all share the intention of a close reciprocal relationship, this cannot occur, for reasons they cannot avoid."

> "Despite goodwill, humans cannot be intimate without the risk of mutual harm, leading to cautious and tentative relationships."

Article, "Social psychological research":

> "The study showed that participants who experienced social exclusion were more likely to seek out new social bonds with others."
