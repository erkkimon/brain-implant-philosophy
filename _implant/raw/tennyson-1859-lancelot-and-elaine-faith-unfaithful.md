# Tennyson 1859 — "Lancelot and Elaine": "faith unfaithful kept him falsely true"

Source: Alfred Tennyson, Idylls of the King, "Lancelot and Elaine" (first
  published 1859), line 877 counting the non-blank lines of the Gutenberg
  text from "Elaine the fair, Elaine the loveable" (editions number lines
  differently).
Original: Project Gutenberg eBook #610, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/610
  (plain text https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/610/pg610.txt) (public domain)
Retrieved: 2026-09-28 (plain text fetched, passage located by grep, lines counted by script)

> "The shackles of an old love straitened him,
>   His honour rooted in dishonour stood,
>   And faith unfaithful kept him falsely true."

Relied on for: the example row from Tennyson.
Context: why Lancelot cannot return Elaine's love — his bond to Guinevere.
Fowler (1926) quotes the last two lines as an example under "oxymoron"
(fowler-1926-modern-english-usage-oxymoron.md).
