> "A classical principle that generally fails intuitionistically may hold in particular situations."  (§2)

> "In every pub there is a person a such that if a drinks then everybody drinks."  (§2)

> "In classical logic, a set satisfies this condition if and only if it is non-empty."  (§2, after the formal definition ∀p ∈ X → Ω ∃a ∈ X (p(a) ⟹ ∀x ∈ X(p(x))))

Relied on for: the empty-domain caveat; the drinker paradox as a property
  of sets studied in constructive settings.
Context: The paper relates the drinker paradox, boolean variants of it,
  searchability and omniscience principles for sets in constructive and
