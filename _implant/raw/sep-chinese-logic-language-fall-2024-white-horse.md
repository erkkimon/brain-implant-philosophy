> "For example, the judgment “A white horse is not a horse” may be deemed false if the salient aspect of our mental focus is on the “horse-ness” feature of each entity indicated (white horse and horse). But it may also be deemed true if the salient aspect is “whiteness”, since whiteness is a feature that may be true of some horses but not others."  (§ on Mohist models)

> "Xunzi’s reference to this example in particular in Book 22 of the Xunzi is likely his response to the paradoxical statement “White horses are not horses”, famously asserted by the sophist Gongsun Long probably as a foil to the Mohists’ theories of naming and reference"  (§ on Xunzi)


Relied on for: Mou's (2016) salience account as Willman reports it, and
Willman's reading of Xunzi Book 22 as a response to Gongsun Long.
Context: the salience passage concerns Mohist criteria of resemblance; the
white-horse judgement is Willman's illustration of Mou's view.
