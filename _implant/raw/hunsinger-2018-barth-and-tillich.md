> "Karl Barth and Paul Tillich stood for two contrary ways of doing Christian theology."

> "Tillich’s theology was philosophically “speculative” and “anthropocentric,” whereas Barth’s was strongly “dogmatic” and “christocentric.”"

> "By his own account Tillich’s method was “apologetic” and “correlationist,” whereas Barth’s method, about which he said rather little directly, was more nearly “exegetical” and “hermeneutical,” marked by a continual, often innovative, wrestling with Holy Scripture."

> "Although the two were not without points of convergence, the “anthropocentric”/“christocentric” divergence between them was decisive. What they finally represented was the divided mind of modern theology."
