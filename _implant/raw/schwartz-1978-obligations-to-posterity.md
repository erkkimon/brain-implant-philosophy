Relied on for: Schwartz's statement of the argument (the "Case of the
  Disappearing Beneficiaries", his section 1 title), his thesis that there
  is no obligation to provide widespread, continuing benefits to distant
  descendants, the exponential-divergence estimate, and his closing
  suggestion that any obligation is owed to contemporaries.
Context: Schwartz frames the argument with population policy ("a severely
  restrictive population policy in preference to a more or less
  laissez-faire policy") and grants "that uncontrolled procreation would
  beget a drop in the quality of human life" (p. 3). "Way 1" and "Way 2"
  (pp. 4–7) are two readings of the claim that the restrictive policy
  would benefit distant descendants; Way 2 concerns the future society as
  a whole.
