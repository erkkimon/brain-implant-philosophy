> "According to the author, no one can help us find God just as no one can help a fish in the sea find the ocean (cf. One Minute Wisdom, 67; Awareness, 103)."  (body; preceded by the Note's statement that de Mello "makes statements about God which ignore his personal nature, if not explicitly denying it")

> "The only difference between Jesus and those others was that he was awake and they weren't" (Awareness, 30-31)  (body; the Note's quotation of de Mello, cited by page)

> "One sign that you're awakened is that you don't give a damn about what's going to happen in the next life. You're not bothered about it; you don't care. You are not interested, period" (Awareness, 42-43, 150).  (body)

> "Why bother about tomorrow? Is there a life after death? Will I survive after death? Why bother about tomorrow? Get into today" (Awareness, 114).  (body)

> "There is no reason to repent for sins, since the only thing that matters is to be awakened to an awareness of reality: "Don't weep for your sins. Why weep for sins that you committed when you were asleep?" (Awareness, 26; cf. ibid., 43, 150)."  (body)

> "Not all the works of Father de Mello were authorized for publication by the author himself. Some were published after his death based on his writings, or on notes or recordings of his conferences."  (note 1)
