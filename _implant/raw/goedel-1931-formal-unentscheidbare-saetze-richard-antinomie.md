German, OCR corrected by hand (reference only): "Die Analogie dieses
Schlusses mit der Antinomie Richard springt in die Augen; auch mit dem
„Lügner" besteht eine nahe Verwandtschaft". The footnote attached to

epistemologische Antinomie zu einem derartigen Unentscheidbarkeitsbeweis

> "The analogy of this conclusion with the Richard-antinomy leaps to the eye; there is also a close kinship with the liar-antinomy, because our undecidable theorem Rq (q) states that q is in K, i.e. according to (1) that Rq (q) is not provable."  (Hirzel translation, §1; subscript lost in text extraction)

> "Hence, we have in front of us a theorem that states its own unprovability."  (Hirzel translation, §1)

Relied on for: Gödel's own comparison of his undecidable sentence with
the Richard antinomy and the liar.
Context: §1 sketches the proof idea: formulas with one free variable
are ordered (Rn), K is the class of n such that Rn(n) is unprovable, and
a formula S defining K is some Rq; Rq(q) is then shown undecidable,
assuming every provable formula is true. Hirzel's translation omits the
footnotes; the footnote above is read only in the German OCR.
