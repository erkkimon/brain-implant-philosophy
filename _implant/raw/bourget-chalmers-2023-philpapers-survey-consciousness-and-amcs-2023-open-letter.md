## B. AMCS open letter (26 April 2023)

Source: Association for Mathematical Consciousness Science, "The
  Responsible Development of AI Agenda Needs to Include Consciousness
  Research", open letter, page-dated "PUBLISHED April 26, 2023"; first
  listed signatories Lenore Blum, Johannes Kleiner, Jonathan Mason,
  Robin Lorenz, Manuel Blum, Yoshua Bengio.
Original: https://amcs-community.org/open-letters/
  (copyrighted; excerpts only)
Retrieved: 2026-09-25 (HTML fetched with curl, HTTP 200)

> "Indeed, it is no longer in the realm of science fiction to imagine AI
>  systems having feelings and even human-level consciousness.
>  Contemporary AI systems already display human traits recognised in
>  Psychology, including evidence of Theory of Mind [2]."  (letter body;
>  [2] is arXiv:2302.02083)

> "Without taking a position on whether AI development should be paused,
>  we emphasise that the rapid development of AI is exposing the urgent
>  need to accelerate research in the field of consciousness science.
>  [...] We call on the tech sector, the scientific community and society
>  as a whole to take seriously the need to accelerate research in
>  consciousness in order to ensure that AI development delivers positive
>  outcomes for humanity. AI research should not be left to wander
>  alone."  ("The way ahead")

Relied on for: the measured distribution of professional philosophers'
views in 2020 — a majority accept a hard problem, a bare majority accept
physicalism, only a quarter accept zombies as metaphysically possible,
functionalism is the plurality theory of consciousness, and 3.39% accept
that current AI systems are conscious versus 39.19% for future AI
systems; the AMCS call as an example of a consciousness-science body's
public position in 2023.
Context: the survey targeted English-language philosophy faculty; the
"Other" category collapses several options (agnostic, no fact of the
matter, alternative view, etc.). The "Other minds" question allowed
independent accept/reject per group, so its rows do not sum to 100%. The
AMCS letter frames its call in response to the Future of Life "Pause
Giant AI Experiments" letter, and its Theory-of-Mind claim rests on a
single preprint (arXiv:2302.02083, Kosinski 2023); the letter does not
itself claim any AI is conscious.
