# Carlin 1975 — Saturday Night Live monologue: "Jumbo Shrimp", "Military Intelligence"

Source: George Carlin, monologue, Saturday Night Live season 1 episode 1,
  11 October 1975, fan transcript at SNL Transcripts (cited by English
  Wikipedia's "Oxymoron" article for the example).
Original: http://snltranscripts.jt.org/75/75acarlin2.phtml (transcript;
  copyrighted performance; excerpt only)
Retrieved: 2026-09-28 (HTML fetched; line located by grep)

> "The term Jumbo Shrimp has always amazed me.  What is a Jumbo Shrimp?  I
> mean, it’s like Military Intelligence – the words don’t go together, man."

Relied on for: the loose, assertive use in its primary instance — two
phrases said to be self-contradictory, one lexically, one as a joke about
the military.
Context: stand-up routine; Carlin does not use the word "oxymoron" in the
transcript read. Wikipedia describes the "military intelligence" case
as implying "that "military" inherently excludes the presence of
"intelligence"" (wikipedia-oxymoron-article-lederer-carlin-oed.md, source
page).
