> "In 1911, he received a doctoral degree in philosophy from the University of Breslau, and earned the licentiate in theology from the University of Halle in 1912."  (§1)

> "In the same year he was called to the University of Marburg in which the neo-orthodoxy theology of Karl Barth was incredibly popular."  (§1, the year is 1924)

> "Tillich met Martin Heidegger at the university and was deeply influenced by his thought."  (§1)

> "Synthesizing what he had learned from Schelling, Kahler and Kierkegaard and Husserl, Tillich reflected himself in his subsequent theological works with tremendous indebtedness to the thought of Existentialism."  (§1)

> "The Courage to Be (1952); Love, Power, and Justice: Ontological Analysis and Ethical Applications (1954);"  (§2)

> "Morality and Beyond (1963);"  (§2)
