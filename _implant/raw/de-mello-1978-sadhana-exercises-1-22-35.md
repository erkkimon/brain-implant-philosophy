> "We are accustomed to think of Scripture as the revelation of God. And so it is. I want you now to discover the revelation that silence brings."  (Exercise 1)

> "This is a form of fantasy prayer recommended by Saint Ignatius of Loyola in his Spiritual Exercises and frequently used by many of the saints."  (Exercise 22)

> "It consists in taking a scene from the life of Christ and reliving it, taking part in it as if it were actually occurring and you were a participant in the event."  (Exercise 22)

> "The ceaseless repetition of the name of Jesus is a form of prayer very dear to Greek and Russian Orthodox Christians"  (Exercise 35; the sentence continues "who find in this form of prayer the firm foundation of their prayer life")

Relied on for: how the Fantasy and Devotion parts introduce two named traditional methods; the Exercise 1 passage the CDF Explanatory Note quotes (knowledge/works/sadhana.md).
Context: Exercise 1 opens with "'Silence is the great revelation,' said Lao-tse." and proceeds to a silence exercise; Exercise 22 then gives a passage from John's gospel to be relived; Exercise 35 recommends a book on the practice.
