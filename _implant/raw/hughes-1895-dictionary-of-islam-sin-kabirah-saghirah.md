# Hughes 1895 — Dictionary of Islam, "Sin": kabīrah and ṣaghīrah

Source: Thomas Patrick Hughes, A Dictionary of Islam (London: W. H. Allen,
  1895), s.v. "Sin", pp. 594–595.
Original: https://archive.org/details/dictionaryislam00hughuoft
  (public domain; scan OCR text read)
Retrieved: 2026-10-15 (djvu OCR text downloaded and grepped)

> "Kabirah, " great," and $aghirah, " little " sins."  (pp. 594–595; the OCR's "$aghirah" stands for ṣaghīrah)

> "Muhammadan writers are not agreed as to the exact number of kabtrah sins"  (pp. 594–595; "kabtrah" is the OCR's)

Relied on for: the division of sins into great (kabīrah) and little
  (ṣaghīrah) as Hughes reports it, and his report that the number of
  great sins is disputed.
Context: Hughes heads the entry "SIN, Arabic zamb ..., khati'ah ism" (the
  OCR's transliteration), says "Muharamadan doctors divde sin into two
  classes" (OCR spelling), lists seventeen great sins as "generally held"
  (beginning with kufr), and quotes traditions from the Mishkāt, book i,
  ch. ii, including one naming as the greatest sin to associate another
  with God. Hughes's terminology ("Muhammadan") is his,
  of 1895.
