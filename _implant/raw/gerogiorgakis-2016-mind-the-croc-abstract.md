# Gerogiorgakis 2016 — "Mind the Croc!" (abstract)

Source: Stamatios Gerogiorgakis, "Mind the Croc! Rationality Gaps vis-à-vis
  the Crocodile Paradox", History and Philosophy of Logic 37(2), 2016,

> "This article discusses rationality gaps triggered by self-referential/cyclic choice, the latter being understood as choosing according to a norm that refers to the choosing itself."

> "The Crocodile Paradox is reformulated and analyzed as a game—named CP—whose Nash equilibrium is shown to trigger a cyclic choice and to invite a rationality gap."

> "It is shown that CP is a counter-example to Gaifman's solution of the rationality gaps problem."

Relied on for: a modern decision- and game-theoretic treatment of the case,
reported from its abstract only.
Context: the abstract also says that choosing the Nash equilibrium of CP
conforms to principles of Wolfgang Spohn and Haim Gaifman meant to
guarantee acyclicity, yet does not prevent cyclic choice.
