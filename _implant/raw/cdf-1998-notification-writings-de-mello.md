# CDF 1998 — Notification concerning the writings of Father Anthony de Mello, SJ

Source: Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, "Notification concerning the writings of Father Anthony de Mello, SJ", Rome, 24 June 1998, signed Joseph Card. Ratzinger (Prefect) and Tarcisio Bertone (Secretary). English text on the Vatican website.

> "The Indian Jesuit priest, Father Anthony de Mello (1931-1987) is well known due to his numerous publications which, translated into various languages, have been widely circulated in many countries of the world, though not all of these texts were authorized by him for publication."  (para. 1)

> "His works, which almost always take the form of brief stories, contain some valid elements of oriental wisdom."  (para. 1)

> "Especially in his early writings, Father de Mello, while revealing the influence of Buddhist and Taoist spiritual currents, remained within the lines of Christian spirituality."  (para. 1)

> "But already in certain passages in these early works and to a greater degree in his later publications, one notices a progressive distancing from the essential contents of the Christian faith."  (para. 2)

> "But he considers Jesus as a master alongside others."  (para. 3)

> "With the present Notification, in order to protect the good of the Christian faithful, this Congregation declares that the above-mentioned positions are incompatible with the Catholic faith and can cause grave harm."  (final para.)

> "Rome, from the offices of the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith, June 24, 1998, the Solemnity of the Birth of John the Baptist."  (dateline)
