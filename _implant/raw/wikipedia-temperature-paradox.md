> "The Temperature paradox or Partee's paradox is a classic puzzle in formal semantics and philosophical logic. Formulated by Barbara Partee in the 1970s, it consists of the following argument, which speakers of English judge as wildly invalid."  (article lead)

> "Despite its obvious invalidity, this argument would be valid in most formalizations based on traditional extensional systems of logic."  (article)
