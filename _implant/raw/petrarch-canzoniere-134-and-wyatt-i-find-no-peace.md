# Petrarch, Canzoniere 134 "Pace non trovo" and Wyatt's version "I find no peace"

Source: Francesco Petrarca, Rerum vulgarium fragmenta (Canzoniere), no. 134
  (134th item in Wikisource's index of the collection), lines 1–4, 9, 12–13;
  Sir Thomas Wyatt, "Description of the Contrarious Passions in a Lover"
  ("I find no peace, and all my war is done"), lines 1–2, text of
  J. Yeowell (ed.), The Poetical Works of Sir Thomas Wyatt (London: George
  Bell and Sons, 1904), pp. 9–10, as reproduced by Luminarium.
Original: https://it.wikisource.org/wiki/Canzoniere_(Rerum_vulgarium_fragmenta)/Pace_non_trovo,_et_non_ò_da_far_guerra
  (read via action=raw; public domain text); https://www.luminarium.org/renlit/ifindno.htm
  (1904 text, public domain; Luminarium site pages copyright A. Jokinen)
Retrieved: 2026-09-28 (both fetched; Petrarch's number checked by counting
  the entries of Wikisource's collection index)

> "Pace non trovo, et non ò da far guerra;
> e temo, et spero; et ardo, et son un ghiaccio;
> et volo sopra ’l cielo, et giaccio in terra;
> et nulla stringo, et tutto ’l mondo abbraccio."  (RVF 134.1–4)

> "Veggio senza occhi, et non ò lingua et grido;"  (RVF 134.9)

> "Pascomi di dolor, piangendo rido;
> egualmente mi spiace morte et vita:"  (RVF 134.12–13)

> "FIND no peace, and all my war is done ;
> I fear and hope, I burn, and freeze like
>       ice ;"  (Wyatt, lines 1–2; the Luminarium page prints the first
>  letter "I" as a separate drop capital and breaks line 2 after "like")
