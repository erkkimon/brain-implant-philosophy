## Terminology: the bundle is `_implant/`, not `_brain/`

Decision (erkkimon): the bundle folder is renamed `_brain/` → `_implant/`
and every rule, page and comment says *implant* where it meant this
repository. The reasoning is in
[Using the brain implant](../skills/using-the-brain-implant.md): the brain
is the user's cortex (their own brainpick brain); this repository is an
implant mounted beside it. Calling the implant a brain blurred the one
distinction the design rests on (G10). brainpick reads the bundle root from
`brainpick.toml`, so the rename is a config line plus `git mv`; henxels
paths in `henxels.yaml` updated, AGENTS.md regenerated, compile artefacts
rebuilt from scratch.

README rewritten with the title *Philosophy Brain Implant for brainpick*:
leads with what the skills do (a table, one row per skill), then the
knowledge graph, then two install paths — clone-and-point, and mount with
brainpick — with the second path explaining what the agent gains (graph
search, skills boosted, federation with the cognitive-tools implant).

## Founding decisions — erkkimon

The philosophy implant is founded as a public brainpick brain implant meant
to make an agent "super smart in philosophy", covering every area, and
measured against Wikipedia on neutrality, amount and quality of
information. The founder's brief, distilled into the givens of the
[manifest](../vision/manifest.md):

* **Neutrality includes selection.** Wikipedia's neutral-point-of-view rule
  governs the sentence; its due-weight rule leaves what gets told to
  editorial judgement. Here admission is mechanical
  ([Selection is a stated rule](../conventions/selection-is-a-stated-rule.md)).
* **Nobody knows what is true — including us.** The implant gives tools to
  find out, never a verdict
  ([Reporting, not endorsing](../conventions/reporting-not-endorsing.md)).
  Comparison and reconstruction are the implant's; evaluation never is.
* **If it is not logic or mathematics, someone else must have said it and
  it is proven with a reference.** No space for opinions; opinions belong
  to the agent using the implant, in its cortex
  ([How claims are graded](../conventions/how-claims-are-graded.md),
  [Every claim carries its receipt](../conventions/every-claim-carries-its-receipt.md)).
* **No peer-review gatekeeping.** Logic is evaluated by individuals and
  exists without authorities; admission is checkability, not approval
  (manifest G2).
* **Transparent vocabulary.** A normative definition — how we use a term
  and why, for coherence and granularity — beside the descriptive ones,
  how others use it ([Words are contracts](../conventions/words-are-contracts.md)).
* **Argumentation, rhetoric, dialectic and persuasion are one spectrum**,
  documented without judgement: the technique and its critique side by
  side; who calls it a fallacy, under which theory, and who does not.
* **Biases must be spotted systematically, with receipts.** A language
  model spots biases fluently but unsystematically and without sources;
  the implant supplies the walk and the citations
  ([Spot persuasion and bias](../skills/spot-persuasion-and-bias.md)).
* **Persuasion is transparent and chosen.** The user picks the allowed
  techniques before the agent writes ([Write persuasively](../skills/write-persuasively.md))
  and how dirty the agent may fight before it argues ([Debate](../skills/debate.md)).
* **Bayesian updating is the recommended tool for an "educated gut
  feeling"**, offered not imposed, its critiques documented beside it; the
  machinery lives in the shared cognitive-tools implant, none here. A
  published example is a *provocation* — numbers to be overwritten.
* **Classical and modern thinkers on one playground**, meeting on the
  problem page; the knowledge graph is problem-centric
  ([knowledge](../knowledge/index.md)).
* **Skills say "brain implant", not "brain"**: a brain is the agent's own
  cortex plus its implants; either alone is slightly lobotomised
  ([Using the brain implant](../skills/using-the-brain-implant.md)).
* **Public from the first commit**, pull requests welcome, no personal
  details; content CC BY-SA 4.0, tooling MIT; decisions attributed to
  "erkkimon" ([Written to be public](../conventions/written-to-be-public.md)).
* **Git**: the agent never stages or commits; the founder reviews.
