## Oxymorons, paradoxes, dilemmas, ethical problems — first cluster

erkkimon asked for oxymorons, paradoxes, dilemmas and ethical problems to be
covered "as soon as possible", while continuing toward the MVP. Decision: this
cluster replaces free will / induction as the v0.1 second exemplar, and the
rest of the area becomes the first breadth section in [open work](../todo/open.md).

Wrote three vocabulary contracts — [oxymoron](../knowledge/vocabulary/oxymoron.md)
(figure vs. loose "contradiction in terms"), [paradox](../knowledge/vocabulary/paradox.md)
(plausible premises and reasoning, unacceptable conclusion) and
[dilemma](../knowledge/vocabulary/dilemma.md) (argument form / loose / moral) —
and four problems: [the trolley problem](../knowledge/problems/trolley-problem.md),
[moral dilemmas](../knowledge/problems/moral-dilemmas.md),
[the sorites](../knowledge/problems/sorites-paradox.md) and
[the liar](../knowledge/problems/liar-paradox.md). Grounded in five new raw/
excerpts: SEP Fall 2024 (McConnell; Woollard; McIntyre; Hyde & Raffman;
Beall, Glanzberg & Ripley; Cantini), the 2009 PhilPapers Survey page, and
Silva Rhetoricae / Wiktionary / Etymonline for the word senses. `logic.py`
checked constructive and destructive dilemma, the agglomeration argument
against dilemmas (premises jointly inconsistent), a sorites chain (valid) and
the liar biconditional (inconsistent, so trivialising).

Findings on the way: the two SEP entries give different page ranges for
Thomson 2008 (201–12 vs 359–374); Crossref gives 359–374. The 2020
PhilPapers results site returned a bot challenge, so only the 2009 trolley
figures are recorded. Plato, *Republic* 331c was checked on Perseus.
