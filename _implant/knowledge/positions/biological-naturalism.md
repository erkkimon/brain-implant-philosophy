# Biological naturalism

A position page of the [positions](index.md) branch, written under
[Reporting, not endorsing](../../conventions/reporting-not-endorsing.md).

## Thesis

"Consciousness is a property of only (but not necessarily all) living
systems" ([Seth 2025](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/tz6an), §4.0;
excerpt: `raw/seth-2025-biological-naturalism-and-conscious-ai.md`).
Distinguished from Searle's original formulation ("conscious states are
higher-level irreducible properties of lower-level neurobiological
states") and from biopsychism ("all living systems are conscious").

## Answers the problem

[What is consciousness, and how does it relate to the physical?](../problems/consciousness.md)
— answers the phenomenal question by tying it to life.

## Canonical statements

- **Seth 2025:** "I'll instead make the case that consciousness depends on
  our nature as living organisms – a form of biological naturalism"
  (abstract). "Real artificial consciousness is unlikely along current
  trajectories, but becomes more plausible as AI becomes more brain-like
  and/or life-like."
- **Searle 1980 (ancestor):** "only very special kinds of machines, namely
  brains and machines with internal causal powers equivalent to those of
  brains" can think (abstract, p. 417; excerpt:
  `raw/searle-1980-minds-brains-programs-chinese-room.md`). Seth uses the
  term differently.

## The case for

- **Predictive processing / free energy.** Seth argues from the free
  energy principle that consciousness is tied to self-maintaining living
  systems (§4.0; details in the excerpt but not fully quoted here).
- **Biases.** Seth lists "the tendency to equate intelligence with
  consciousness" as a bias to avoid (§2; context note in the seth-2025
  raw file).
- **Chalmers 2023:** biology is one of six obstacles; "at least a
  one-in-three chance … that biology is required for consciousness" (§4;
  excerpt: `raw/chalmers-2023-could-an-llm-be-conscious.md`).

## The case against

- **Computational functionalism.** Butlin et al. 2023 adopt it as working
  hypothesis; biological naturalism rejects it.
- **Dehaene, Lau & Kouider 2017:** "the empirical evidence is compatible
  with the possibility that consciousness arises from nothing more than
  specific computations" (p. 492).
- **Substrate independence intuitions.** Not quoted in the excerpts held.

## Replies

Seth 2025: "If we sell our minds too cheaply to our machine creations, we
not only overestimate them – we underestimate ourselves" (abstract).

## Who holds it

Seth 2025; Searle (original formulation, different sense). Not widely
adopted as a named position outside Seth's work.

## Contrasts

- **[Functionalism](functionalism.md):** substrate-neutral vs
  substrate-specific.
- **[Integrated information theory](integrated-information-theory.md):**
  IIT ties consciousness to informational structure; biological naturalism
  to living organization.
- **[Global workspace](global-workspace.md):** GW is compatible with
  non-biological instantiation; biological naturalism is not.
