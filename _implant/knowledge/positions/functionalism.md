# Functionalism

A position page of the [positions](index.md) branch, written under
[Reporting, not endorsing](../../conventions/reporting-not-endorsing.md)
and graded by [How claims are graded](../../conventions/how-claims-are-graded.md).

## Thesis

"What makes something a mental state of a given type does not depend on
its internal constitution but solely on its function, or the role it plays"
(SEP "Functionalism" Fall 2024, §1, reporting Putnam 1967; excerpt:
`raw/putnam-1967-psychological-predicates-via-sep.md`). In AI-facing form:
"performing computations of the right kind is necessary and sufficient
for consciousness" — adopted by Butlin, Long et al. 2023 "as a working
hypothesis … for pragmatic reasons" (executive summary, p. 3; excerpt:
`raw/butlin-long-2023-ai-consciousness-indicators.md`).

## Answers the problem

[What is consciousness, and how does it relate to the physical?](../problems/consciousness.md)
— answers the access question directly; the phenomenal question only if
one adds that phenomenal states are functional states.

## Canonical statements

- **Putnam 1967:** mental states are functional states, multiply realizable
  across creatures (SEP §1, §3.1; excerpt: `raw/putnam-1967-psychological-predicates-via-sep.md`).
- **Butlin et al. 2023:** "computational functionalism" as working
  hypothesis; indicator properties derived from neuroscientific theories
  (Table 1, p. 5).

## Varieties

Machine functionalism, psychofunctionalism, analytic functionalism, role
functionalism — none distinguished in the excerpts held.

## The case for

- **Multiple realizability.** Putnam: pain cannot be identified with C-fiber
  firing because "a wide variety of terrestrial creatures seemingly capable
  of experiencing pain" have different physiologies (SEP §3.1).
- **AI tractability.** Butlin et al.: computational functionalism "entails
  that consciousness in AI is possible in principle and that studying the
  workings of AI systems is relevant" (executive summary, p. 3).
- **Computational sufficiency.** Dehaene, Lau & Kouider 2017: "the
  empirical evidence is compatible with the possibility that consciousness
  arises from nothing more than specific computations" (p. 492; excerpt:
  `raw/dehaene-lau-kouider-2017-c1-c2-machines.md`).

## The case against

- **Block's phenomenal/access distinction.** "Phenomenal consciousness is
  experience … The mark of access-consciousness, by contrast, is
  availability for use in reasoning"; conflating them produces bad results
  ([Block 1995](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X00038188), abstract;
  excerpt: `raw/block-1995-phenomenal-and-access-consciousness.md`).
  Functionalism explains access; whether it explains phenomenal
  consciousness is the dispute.
- **Searle's Chinese room.** "Instantiating a computer program is never by
  itself a sufficient condition of intentionality"
  ([Searle 1980](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X00005756), abstract,
  p. 417; excerpt: `raw/searle-1980-minds-brains-programs-chinese-room.md`).
- **IIT's true zombies.** "There can be true 'zombies' – unconscious
  feed-forward systems that are functionally equivalent to conscious
  complexes" (Oizumi, Albantakis & Tononi 2014, abstract; excerpt:
  `raw/oizumi-albantakis-tononi-2014-iit-3-axioms.md`).
- **Biological naturalism.** Seth 2025: "consciousness depends on our
  nature as living organisms"; "the fact that computational functionalism
  is frequently assumed does not mean it is true" (abstract; excerpt:
  `raw/seth-2025-biological-naturalism-and-conscious-ai.md`).

## Replies

Not in the excerpts held for Putnam specifically. Dennett's Multiple Drafts
Model is a functionalist reply to qualia-based objections (SEP
"Consciousness" §9.4; excerpt: `raw/dennett-1991-consciousness-explained-via-sep.md`).

## Who holds it

Survey 2020: functionalism 33.04 % (plurality; N = 1020; Bourget &
Chalmers 2023). Thinkers: Putnam, Fodor, Lewis, Block (as critic);
Butlin et al. adopt it pragmatically.

## Contrasts

- **[Physicalism / identity theory](physicalism.md):** functionalism
  replaces type-identity with role-definition.
- **[Dualism](dualism.md):** functionalism is substrate-neutral; dualism
  requires non-physical substance or properties.
- **[Illusionism](illusionism.md):** denies the phenomenal properties
  functionalism tries to functionalize.
- **[Integrated information theory](integrated-information-theory.md):**
  rejects functional equivalence as sufficient.
- **[Biological naturalism](biological-naturalism.md):** rejects
  computation as sufficient.
