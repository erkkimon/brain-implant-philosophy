# Integrated information theory

A position page of the [positions](index.md) branch, written under
[Reporting, not endorsing](../../conventions/reporting-not-endorsing.md).

## Thesis

"Consciousness corresponds to the capacity of a system to integrate
information" ([Tononi 2004](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2202-5-42),
abstract; excerpt: `raw/tononi-2004-integrated-information.md`). From 2014:
"IIT arrives at an identity: an experience is a maximally irreducible
conceptual structure (MICS)" and "integrated information ΦMax its quantity"
(Oizumi, Albantakis & Tononi 2014, abstract; excerpt:
`raw/oizumi-albantakis-tononi-2014-iit-3-axioms.md`).

## Answers the problem

[What is consciousness, and how does it relate to the physical?](../problems/consciousness.md)
— addresses the hard problem directly (Seth & Bayne 2022 classification).

## Canonical statements

Five axioms (Oizumi et al. 2014): Existence ("I experience therefore I
am"), Composition, Information, Integration, Exclusion. Each followed by
postulates about physical substrates. "Axioms are self-evident truths
about consciousness … Postulates instead are assumptions about the physical
world" (section "Axioms and postulates").

## The case for

- **Phenomenological grounding.** Axioms derived from properties of
  experience itself, not from behaviour or neural correlates.
- **Quantitative measure.** Φ provides a measurable quantity of
  consciousness.
- **Clinical application.** Koch, Massimini, Boly & Tononi 2016: the
  posterior cortical "hot zone" as the neural correlate predicted by IIT
  (excerpt: `raw/koch-massimini-boly-tononi-2016-ncc-hot-zone.md`).

## The case against

- **Cogitate 2025 adversarial test:** "a lack of sustained synchronization
  within the posterior cortex contradicts the claim that network
  connectivity specifies consciousness"
  ([Cogitate 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1), abstract;
  excerpt: `raw/cogitate-2025-adversarial-test-and-fleming-2023-pseudoscience-letter.md`).
- **Fleming et al. 2023 open letter:** classifies IIT "as pseudoscience";
  the letter carries its own header note: "this is not the final version"
  (p. 1; same excerpt file). ~120 signatories.
- **Butlin et al. 2023:** IIT excluded from indicator derivation because
  "not compatible with computational functionalism" (executive summary).
- **True zombies.** Oizumi et al. 2014: "there can be true 'zombies' –
  unconscious feed-forward systems that are functionally equivalent to
  conscious complexes" (abstract). This bears directly on AI: standard
  digital architectures may have Φ ≈ 0.

## Who holds it

Tononi, Oizumi, Albantakis, Koch, Massimini, Boly. Not widely adopted
outside the IIT research community.

## Contrasts

- **[Global workspace](global-workspace.md):** GW focuses on broadcast;
  IIT on integration. Cogitate tested both head-to-head.
- **[Functionalism](functionalism.md):** IIT rejects functional equivalence
  as sufficient (true zombies).
- **[Biological naturalism](biological-naturalism.md):** Seth's view ties
  consciousness to living systems; IIT ties it to informational structure
  regardless of substrate.
