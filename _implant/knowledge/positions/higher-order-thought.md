# Higher-order thought theory

A position page of the [positions](index.md) branch, written under
[Reporting, not endorsing](../../conventions/reporting-not-endorsing.md).

## Thesis

"A conscious mental state M, of mine, is a state that is actually causing
an activated thought (generally a non-conscious one) that I have M, and
causing it non-inferentially" (SEP "Higher-Order Theories of Consciousness"
Fall 2024, §4, reporting Rosenthal; excerpt:
`raw/rosenthal-2005-higher-order-thought-via-sep.md`). The general form:
"a phenomenally conscious mental state is a mental state … that either is,
or is disposed to be, the object of a higher-order representation" (§2).

## Answers the problem

[What is consciousness, and how does it relate to the physical?](../problems/consciousness.md)
— answers the phenomenal question by reducing it to a relational property.

## Canonical statements

- **Rosenthal (via SEP):** the Transitivity Principle — "a conscious state
  is a state whose subject is, in some way, aware of being in it" (§2).
- **Locke 1690 (ancestor):** "it being impossible for any one to perceive
  without perceiving that he does perceive" (*Essay* II.xxvii.9; excerpt:
  `raw/locke-1690-essay-2-27-person-and-consciousness.md`). Whether Locke
  is a HOT theorist is disputed; the structural parallel is noted as a
  structural note the implant adds (manifest [G4](../../vision/manifest.md)).

## Varieties

Actualist HOT (Rosenthal): requires an actual higher-order thought.
Dispositional HOT: requires only a disposition to produce one.
Higher-order perception (inner sense): the higher-order state is
perceptual rather than thought-like. Distinctions from SEP §3–§4.

## The case for

- **Transitivity intuition.** The TP captures a pre-theoretic datum about
  consciousness (SEP §2).
- **Metacognition.** Butlin et al. 2023 list HOT indicators (HOT-1 through
  HOT-4) including "metacognitive monitoring distinguishing reliable
  perceptual representations from noise" (Table 1, p. 5; excerpt:
  `raw/butlin-long-2023-ai-consciousness-indicators.md`).

## The case against

Not quoted in the excerpts held. Standard objections in the literature:
misrepresentation problems (a HOT could misrepresent a first-order state),
the problem of unconscious HOTs, and the regress worry. None grounded in
this batch's raw files.

## Contrasts

- **[Global workspace](global-workspace.md):** GW explains consciousness
  via broadcast; HOT via metarepresentation. Different explanatory targets
  (Seth & Bayne 2022).
- **[Integrated information theory](integrated-information-theory.md):**
  IIT addresses the hard problem directly; HOT addresses state-consciousness.
- **[Functionalism](functionalism.md):** HOT is a species of functionalism
  about consciousness specifically.
