# René Descartes

## Facts a claim depends on

1596–1650. French. The excerpts held do not establish dates or places from
a primary reference; these are standard biographical facts. SEP "Descartes"
(Fall 2024) was not fetched for this page; the facts above are given as

## Problems addressed

- **[Consciousness](../problems/consciousness.md):** [dualism](../positions/dualism.md).
  *Meditations* II and VI (1641): "I am a thinking thing"; mind and body
  are really distinct. *Discourse* Part V (1637): two tests separate
  thinking beings from machines. Both excerpted in
  `raw/descartes-1641-meditations-thinking-thing-real-distinction.md` and
  `raw/descartes-1637-discourse-two-tests-for-machines.md`.

## In dialogue with

- **Locke 1690** redefined personal identity in terms of consciousness
  rather than substance (*Essay* II.xxvii.9; excerpt:
  `raw/locke-1690-essay-2-27-person-and-consciousness.md`).
- **Leibniz 1714** answered the machine question differently: perception
  "must be sought for" in a simple substance (*Monadology* §17; excerpt:
  `raw/leibniz-1714-monadology-17-mill-argument.md`).
- **Ryle 1949** named Descartes' view "the dogma of the Ghost in the
  Machine" and called it "one big mistake … a category-mistake" (ch. I;
  excerpt: `raw/ryle-1949-concept-of-mind-ghost-in-the-machine.md`).
  Ryle's phrase is his own "deliberate abusiveness".
- **Turing 1950** replaced the language test with the imitation game
  (§1; excerpt: `raw/turing-1950-computing-machinery-imitation-game-consciousness.md`).
  Whether Turing intended this as a reply to Descartes specifically is not
  established in the excerpts held; the structural parallel is noted as a
  structural note the implant adds (manifest [G4](../../vision/manifest.md)).
- **Oizumi, Albantakis & Tononi 2014** paraphrase Descartes: "'I experience
  therefore I am'" as IIT's existence axiom (abstract; excerpt:
  `raw/oizumi-albantakis-tononi-2014-iit-3-axioms.md`).
- **Chinese xin (contrast).** Wong, SEP "Mind (Heart-Mind) in Chinese
  Philosophy" (Fall 2024): "Unlike Descartes' mind, xin's qi can interact
  with both the body's qi and the wider qi" (§1.2; excerpt:
  `raw/sep-chinese-mind-fall-2024-heart-mind-and-body.md`).
- **Strawson 2006:** "'substance dualism', a view for which (as Arnauld
  saw) there has never been any good argument" (p. 26; excerpt:
  `raw/strawson-2006-realistic-monism.md`).

## Reception

- **For:** Oizumi et al. adopt the cogito as an axiom. Leibniz builds on
  the simple-substance thesis.
- **Against:** Ryle's category-mistake charge; Strawson's "never been any
  good argument"; the Chinese xin tradition as a non-dualist alternative.
- **Survey:** dualism 21.96 %, non-physicalism 32.08 % (Bourget & Chalmers
  2023).
