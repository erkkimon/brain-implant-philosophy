# Vocabulary

The term contract. Truth can be carried by any vocabulary provided the
meanings are transparent, definite, and granular enough for the piece of
reality being talked about — so this implant fixes, for each term it relies
on, a **normative** definition (how the term is used here, and why it is
split from its neighbours the way it is) and sets beside it the
**descriptive** definitions: how the term is actually used, by whom, cited
([Words are contracts](../../conventions/words-are-contracts.md)).

Neither is hidden. The normative choice is a decision this implant made for
coherence and is recorded as one; it is never a claim that other uses are
wrong.

The definition used here. Why this one: what it is split from, what
granularity it needs to carry, which pages depend on it. The journal entry

## Descriptive definitions
One entry per distinct sense in use: the sense, who uses it (thinker,
school, discipline, ordinary usage), the source with locator.

## Collisions
Where two senses are commonly confused, and which pages in this implant are
affected.

Linked, with the boundary between them stated.
