## Cited for

These claims come from SEP "Consciousness" (Van Gulick, Fall 2024), as
excerpted in the raw file:

- The **Multiple Drafts Model**. SEP §9.4: "The most prominent
  philosophical example is the Multiple Drafts Model (MDM) of
  consciousness, advanced by Daniel Dennett (1991)."
- The **rejection of the "Cartesian Theater"**. SEP §9.4 says the MDM
  denies a "privileged spatial or functional location—the so called
  'Cartesian Theater'". What makes a content conscious is instead "what
  Dennett calls 'cerebral celebrity'".
- **Qualia**. SEP says the MDM "denies the existence of qualia" (§9.4).
  See [qualia](../vocabulary/qualia.md) and
  [illusionism](../positions/illusionism.md).

## Interpretations

- SEP §7 classes Dennett among eliminativists who are "more qualified in
  their negative assessment". They take "issue only with some of the
  prominent features" of consciousness, such as qualia and the Cartesian
  Theater. It does not class him among those "rejecting the notion
  outright".
