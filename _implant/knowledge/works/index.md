# Works

Primary texts, one page each, whose job is to make a citation **resolve in
any edition**: the canonical locator scheme (Stephanus pages for Plato,
Bekker numbers for Aristotle, A/B pagination for Kant's first *Critique*,
sutra and verse numbers, section numbers…), the standard editions, and where
a free, public-domain text can be read.

No work is summarised in the implant's voice. What a work *says* is quoted;
what it *means* is reported as the interpretation of a named scholar.

## Works
