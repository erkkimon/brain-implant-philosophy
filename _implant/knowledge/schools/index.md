# Schools

Traditions and movements — Stoicism, Nyāya, Mohism, scholasticism,
phenomenology, analytic philosophy, Ubuntu, Kyoto School — under one
admission rule and one template, whatever the region or period. A school is
admitted when a citable source names it as a school and names members.

## Page template

```
---
type: article
about: organization
title: <name>
description: "<one sentence: when, where, what unites the members>"
tags: [school, <region>, <era>]
timestamp: <ISO 8601>
---

# <name>

## Facts a claim depends on
Period, region, founding figures — cited.

## Defining commitments
The positions (linked) that a source says members share, with the source.
Where members disagree on a defining question, say which members and where.

## Members
Thinkers (linked), each with the citation that places them in the school.

## Problems characteristic of the school
Linked.

## Contrasts and exchanges
Schools it defined itself against or exchanged arguments with, with the
arguments linked.

## Reception
Attributed assessments only.
```
