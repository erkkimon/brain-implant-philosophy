# Conventions

Standing rules for how work is done here — naming, process, contracts the
implant's keepers hold themselves to. One kebab-case page per rule,
`type: convention`, listed here. Not a specific piece of work (that is
`plans/`), not a step-by-step procedure (that is `skills/`) — the standing
answer, applied broadly. Every one derives from a given of the
[manifest](../vision/manifest.md).

## Conventions

Read these first — a rule constrains what every other read is for.

* [Reporting, not endorsing](reporting-not-endorsing.md) — we prove that a
  named thinker, school or text said something; we never say, in our own
  voice, that it is *true*, *false*, *fallacious*, *fringe* or *settled*. Evaluative
  words occur only inside attributed reports and a lint flags them
  elsewhere; every position page carries a case for and a case against
  that each side would sign.
* [Selection is a stated rule](selection-is-a-stated-rule.md) — what gets
  told biases as much as how, so admission is mechanical: a named
  proponent or source in a citable published place. Proportion is
  reported as data, never used to omit; every tradition enters under the
  same rule; coverage is counted against Wikipedia's own lists.
* [Words are contracts](words-are-contracts.md) — each term carries a
  normative definition (how this implant uses it, and why it is split so)
  beside the descriptive definitions of how others use it, cited; the
  normative choice is a recorded decision, never a claim that other uses
  are wrong.
* [How claims are graded](how-claims-are-graded.md) — every sentence is
  textual, interpretive, attributive, empirical, logical or evaluative,
  and each kind needs something before it may appear: a locator, a
  scholar, a source, a graded study with its replication record, a
  checkable reconstruction, or an owner.
* [Every claim carries its receipt](every-claim-carries-its-receipt.md) —
  no statement of fact without a resolvable citation at that statement;
  primary texts by canonical locator so the citation resolves in any
  edition; the fact is cheap, the source is the product.
* [Evidence before authority](evidence-before-authority.md) — a claim is
  weighed by the evidence behind it, never by who made it; consensus is
  data with a source, not a reason; criticism lands on the argument;
  reception sections hold only attributed assessments.
* [Written to be public](written-to-be-public.md) — public from the first
  commit and written for a stranger: no host names, internal paths,
  personal details or credentials; pull requests welcome.
* [Raw holds excerpts, not copies](raw-holds-excerpts-not-copies.md) —
  `raw/` stores only the minimum excerpt needed to support a citation,
  beside a resolvable pointer to the original; translations are
  separately copyrighted even when the original is not.
