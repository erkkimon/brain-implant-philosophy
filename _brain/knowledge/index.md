# Knowledge

Semantic memory — evergreen concept docs, one concept per kebab-case page.
Ground every claim inline (a canonical locator in a primary text, a DOI, a
pinned URL, or say in words that it is a logical step the reader can check).
When a concept becomes a procedure that works, distil it into `../skills/`
and point here to the skill.

The graph is **problem-centric**: the pivot is the question, not the thinker
or the era, so that a classical and a modern thinker who answer the same
question sit on the same page. Every page of one kind has the same shape (its
branch index gives the template), so an agent knows what it will find before
it reads.

## Branches

* **[Problems](problems/index.md)** — the questions of philosophy ("what is
  knowledge?", "is the will free?"), each listing the positions taken on it.
  Start here to compare thinkers.
* **[Positions](positions/index.md)** — the answers, the *-isms*: thesis in
  the proponents' own terms, the case for, the case against, who holds it.
* **[Arguments](arguments/index.md)** — named arguments and thought
  experiments as numbered premises and a conclusion, with who disputes which
  premise.
* **[Thinkers](thinkers/index.md)** — people, classical and modern, one page
  each, linked to the problems they answered and to everyone else who
  answered the same problem.
* **[Works](works/index.md)** — primary texts with their canonical locator
  scheme (Stephanus, Bekker, A/B pagination, sutra numbers…), so a citation
  resolves in any edition.
* **[Schools](schools/index.md)** — traditions and movements, across every
  region and period under the same admission rule.
* **[Persuasion](persuasion/index.md)** — techniques of argumentation,
  rhetoric, dialectic and persuasion on one spectrum: mechanism, who classes
  it as a fallacy and under which theory, who classes it as legitimate, how
  to spot it, how to answer it.
* **[Biases](biases/index.md)** — cognitive biases and heuristics with their
  canonical study, replication status, and the critique of the programme
  that named them.
* **[Vocabulary](vocabulary/index.md)** — the term contract: for each term
  the normative definition this implant uses (and why it is split the way it
  is) beside the descriptive definitions others use, cited.
* **[Methods](methods/index.md)** — tools for thinking: logics, Bayesian
  updating, thought experiments, reflective equilibrium, conceptual
  analysis — each with its case against.
