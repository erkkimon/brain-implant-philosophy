# Problems

The questions of philosophy, one page each. A problem page is the **hub** of
the graph: it is where a classical and a modern thinker who answered the same
question meet, and it is where an agent starts when asked to compare them.

A problem is admitted when it has been posed under a recognisable name in a
citable source. It is *not* answered here — the answers are
[positions](../positions/index.md), and the page lists them without ranking.

```
# <title>

## The question
What is asked, in the terms of the [vocabulary](../vocabulary/index.md);
which term contracts the question depends on.

## Why it matters
What other problems or practices turn on the answer (cited to whoever says
so).

## Positions taken
Every position on record, one line each, linking to its page — grouped by
family if the literature groups them, in the literature's own grouping
(cite it). Proportions where surveyed (e.g. the PhilPapers survey), cited.

## Arguments in play
Links to the argument pages that bear on the problem.

## Thinkers who addressed it
Chronological, every era and tradition, one line each: the thinker, the
position they took (or the question they reframed), the work and locator.
This list is what puts classical and modern on the same playground.

## Framings and reframings
Where a thinker or school has argued the question is malformed, dissolved,
or should be replaced — reported, attributed.

## Vocabulary
The terms whose contract this page depends on, each linked.
```
